# YouTube Hook Samples

## Hook 1 — Curiosity

What if everything you've been told about growing on YouTube is wrong?

## Hook 2 — Problem

You're uploading videos consistently, but your views are still stuck. Here's why that might be happening.

## Hook 3 — Story

Three months ago, this creator had almost no audience. Then one simple change completely transformed their videos.

## Hook 4 — Question

Why do some small creators get thousands of views while others struggle to reach 100?

## Hook 5 — Strong Statement

Your first 10 seconds can decide whether someone watches your entire video—or scrolls away.

---

## Writing Approach

My hooks focus on curiosity, clear problems, storytelling,
and strong reasons for the viewer to keep watching.
